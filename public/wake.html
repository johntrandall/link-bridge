Wake it on the Mac

Nothing happens until you press the button. It asks the bridge on the Mac to run the command shown above: watchbill resume starts the seat that is asleep (a Claude Code session in its own herdr tab, in its system's workspace, with its whole conversation) and refuses when nothing of that name is asleep there, when the seat is live, or when the system is unknown. This is the only bridge button that starts an agent, so it never fires on its own and has no herdr-jump:// scheme form that another page or app could emit.
