Reveal in Finder on the Mac Open in Obsidian on the Mac

Reveal selects the folder in a Finder window and opens nothing. Open in Obsidian shows the folder in the vault that holds it (a note inside it opens and the file explorer expands to it); a folder outside every registered vault is refused with a notification. Both need the Reveal in Finder handler app on this Mac; the first time, Chrome asks whether this site may open it; tick Always allow.
